Optimal setup

Crafting stats — your numbers per compression level

Each raw producer(Bits/Ingots) needs only a time (in seconds) per level. Each craft needs its input cost(s) and a time. Leave a level blank if you can't compress that high yet — the optimizer just won't use it. Compression level = units produced per cycle (e.g. 256× makes 256 units).

Steady-state model: the plan guarantees every input is produced at least as fast as it's consumed, so it runs forever without re-shuffling. Seed a small starting stockpile to smooth the batch timing. Frames assume Bits are pre-produced. All math runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
